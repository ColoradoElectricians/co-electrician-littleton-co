Local planning matters

Plan electrical work around the property—not guesswork

Every property has a different electrical history. A newer home with a few added devices may need a straightforward circuit addition, while an older property or major remodel may require a broader review of wiring, grounding, panel capacity and protection.

If your project is within Littleton city limits, check the city's current permit and review guidance before work begins. Permit requirements can depend on the type of work and the property's jurisdiction, so verify the details for your address.

Helpful tip: Before calling, note what is happening, when it started, which rooms or circuits are affected, and whether any breaker trips. Do not touch exposed wiring or a panel that appears damaged or overheated.

For broader service information, visit the Colorado Electricians homepage or get in touch to discuss your project.

When should you call?

Contact an electrician when you notice recurring problems or need electrical work planned. Take burning smells, sparks, hot outlets or buzzing panels seriously.

  • Breakers trip repeatedly or will not reset
  • Lights flicker across multiple rooms
  • Outlets feel warm or show discoloration
  • You are planning a remodel or new appliance
  • You want to add an EV charger or dedicated circuit

If there is smoke, fire or immediate danger, move away and call emergency services. Do not attempt to investigate live electrical equipment yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Quick guidance for homeowners and businesses considering electrical work in Littleton.

What electrical problems should be checked promptly?

Repeated breaker trips, burning smells, buzzing outlets, warm switch plates, flickering lights or unexplained power loss deserve prompt assessment. If there is smoke or an immediate hazard, move away and call emergency services.

Can an electrician help plan a panel upgrade?

Yes. An electrician can review the existing panel, electrical demand, planned appliances and available capacity to determine whether an upgrade or other changes may be appropriate.

Can I add an EV charger at home in Littleton?

A qualified electrician can assess the panel, circuit capacity, charger location and installation requirements before installing a home charging station. Explore our EV charger installation information.

Do electrical projects in Littleton require permits?

Requirements depend on the work and the property's jurisdiction. Check the City of Littleton's current permit guidance and confirm requirements before starting.

Can lighting be upgraded without remodeling an entire room?

Often, yes. Fixture replacement, task lighting or carefully planned exterior lighting can improve usability without changing the whole room. The wiring and fixture requirements should be assessed first.

What information should I have ready before requesting service?

Describe the symptom, when it occurs, the affected rooms or equipment, and any recent changes. Photos of the fixture or affected area may help, but do not remove covers or expose wiring to take them.
